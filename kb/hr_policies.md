# HR Policies

## Casual leave entitlement

```yaml

domain: HR
topic: leave
title: Casual leave entitlement
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-01-14
```

Every permanent employee gets **8 days of casual leave** per leave year. The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Casual leave is meant for short, personal and often unplanned needs, such as a family errand or a personal appointment.

Rules:

- A single casual leave request can cover at most **3 consecutive working days**. For a longer absence, use earned leave.
- Half-day casual leave is allowed and counts as 0.5 day.
- Casual leave does **not** carry over. Unused days lapse on 31 March and are not paid out.
- Joiners get a pro-rated entitlement: 8 days multiplied by the months remaining in the leave year divided by 12, rounded up to the nearest half day.
- Casual leave cannot be attached directly before or after earned leave for a total of more than 3 consecutive days off, unless your manager approves.

Casual leave needs your manager's approval, ideally 2 working days ahead. In a genuine emergency you can apply on the same day, but you must tell your manager by 10:00. Apply in the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example under **Leave**; the steps are in HR-LEAVE-04 (Apply for leave in the people portal).

Casual leave is available from your first day, including during probation.

## Sick leave and medical certificates

```yaml

```

You get **10 days of sick leave** per leave year (1 April to 31 March). Use it when you are too unwell to work or must care for a sick immediate family member.

Notifying and applying:

- Tell your manager by **10:00 on the first day** by phone or Slack. Do not rely on email alone.
- Record the leave in the people portal as soon as you are back, or within 2 working days if you are away longer.

Medical certificates:

- Up to **2 consecutive working days** of sick leave need no certificate.
- From the **3rd consecutive day**, you must upload a certificate from a registered doctor in the portal. It must cover the full period and should show the dates. It does not need to state the diagnosis.
- A certificate is also required for any sick day immediately before or after a weekend or public holiday if the total absence reaches 3 days.

Unused sick leave does not carry over, and it is not paid out when you leave. If you have used all 10 days and still need time off, your manager and the People Team can agree to charge the extra days to earned leave. If you are away for a long illness, contact the People Team on extension 4200 so they can explain your options in confidence.

## Earned leave accrual and carry-over

```yaml

domain: HR
topic: leave
title: Earned leave accrual and carry-over
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-03-18
```

Earned leave is the main leave for holidays and longer breaks. You earn **1.5 days per month**, which is **18 days per leave year**, credited on the last day of each month.

Key rules:

- Earned leave accrues from your first month but, if you are on probation, can only be used after confirmation (see HR-PROB-02, Taking leave during probation).
- You can carry forward up to **30 days** into the next leave year. Anything above 30 days at 31 March lapses.
- On leaving the company, unused earned leave up to **30 days** is paid out at your daily basic pay rate, which is annual basic divided by 365.
- Weekends and public holidays that fall *within* a block of earned leave are not counted as leave days.
- Leave for a block of 5 or more working days should be applied for **at least 2 weeks ahead**, so your manager can plan cover.
- You cannot take more earned leave than you have accrued; there is no advance leave.

Check your balance in the people portal under **Leave**, then **Balances**. The balance includes days credited so far this year and days carried over.

If the balance looks wrong, raise it with the People Team at people@smartdesk.example with the dates you believe are mis-counted. Corrections are made within 3 working days.

5. Attach a document where one is needed, such as a medical certificate.

If an approved leave is missing from the calendar, refresh the page; it syncs every 15 minutes. If you are unable to log in, see the help desk on extension 4100.

Remember that leave is not final until the status shows **Approved**. Do not book non-refundable travel before then.

## Falling sick during earned leave, and how half-days count

```yaml
id: HR-LEAVE-05
domain: HR
topic: leave
title: Falling sick during earned leave, and how half-days count
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-05-19
```

This entry covers two common questions.

**If I fall sick while on earned leave, can I convert the days to sick leave?** Yes, with conditions:

- You must tell your manager on the first day you are sick, by 10:00.
- You must have **3 or more consecutive sick days**, and you must upload a medical certificate covering them within 2 working days of returning.
- The People Team then changes those days from earned to sick leave and restores the earned leave to your balance. Fewer than 3 days, or a missing certificate, stay as earned leave.
- Conversion is limited to the sick leave you still have. Any days beyond that remain earned leave.

**How do half-days count?**

- A half-day counts as 0.5 day of whatever leave type you choose.
- Two half-days on different dates count as two separate half-days, not one full day.
- A half-day at the start or end of a block of earned leave is allowed.
- A half-day of leave means you work the other half; you cannot combine a half-day of leave with another half-day of a different leave type on the same day unless the People Team approves it.

**Weekends in between.** A weekend or public holiday inside a block of leave is not counted. A weekend between two separate leave requests is also not counted, but your manager may ask whether the two requests should be one.

SmartDesk AI provides paid maternity leave on full pay to employees who give birth.

- **First and second child:** **26 weeks**. Up to 8 of those weeks can be taken before the expected delivery date, and the rest after.
- **Third child onward:** **12 weeks**, of which up to 6 weeks can be taken before the expected delivery date.
- **Miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy:** **6 weeks**, starting from the date it happens, with a medical certificate.

Eligibility: you must have worked at least **80 days** in the 12 months before the expected delivery date. Probation does not stop you qualifying if you meet the 80-day condition.

How to apply:

1. Tell your manager and the People Team (extension 4200) as early as you comfortably can, ideally **8 weeks before** your planned start date.
2. Apply under **Leave**, then **Maternity** in the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example and upload the medical certificate with the expected delivery date.
3. The People Team confirms your dates and your cover plan within 5 working days.

If your baby arrives earlier than planned, tell the People Team as soon as you can and the leave starts from the date of birth. You can add earned leave or unused casual leave directly after maternity leave if you wish. Leave taken is not treated as a break in service, and your reviews and stock-option vesting continue during leave.

## Compensatory off for extra hours

```yaml
id: HR-LEAVE-08
domain: HR
topic: leave
title: Compensatory off for extra hours
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-08-25
```

A **compensatory off (comp-off)** is a day off in return for approved work done on a weekend or public holiday, or for an extended period of work well beyond normal hours, for example during a customer outage.

Rules:

- Comp-off is **not automatic**. Your manager must approve the extra work in advance, or confirm it in writing within 2 working days afterwards.
- Working a full day on a weekend or public holiday earns **1 comp-off day**. Four to six hours earns a half-day.
- After approval you must **record the credit in the people portal** under **Leave**, then **Comp-off credit**. An email approval alone is not enough, and the day cannot be taken until the portal shows the credit as approved.
- A comp-off day must be used within **45 days** of the date it was earned. After that it lapses and cannot be carried forward or paid out.
- Comp-off can be combined with other leave, but not with more than **3 leave types** in one continuous absence, for example comp-off with casual and earned leave.

Interpretation points:

- Normal overtime on weekdays, for example staying an extra hour, does not earn comp-off. Teams are expected to plan work to fit regular hours.
- If you forget to record the credit within the 45 days, the day is lost, even if your manager approved the work.
- Extended hours during a major incident may be compensated more flexibly at the discretion of your department head; ask your manager.

## Hybrid schedule and core office days

```yaml

```

SmartDesk AI works a hybrid model. The core office days are **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday** in the Bengaluru or Pune office. You may work from home on **up to 2 days a week** without special approval, normally Monday and Friday.

Expectations when working from home:

- **Availability:** be reachable on chat, phone and email during the core hours of **10:00 to 17:00 IST**. Join scheduled meetings by video.
- **Devices and data:** use only your company laptop for company work. Personal devices must not be used to open sensitive company data. Connect to the VPN for internal systems.
- **Your workplace:** work from a place where you can keep company information private, and be able to take calls without being overheard.
- **Absence:** for planned leave, tell your manager and record it in the people portal. For an unplanned absence, such as an emergency, tell your manager as early as possible.
- **Breaks:** take short breaks away from your screen through the day and manage your workload with your manager. If work is affecting your health, the People Team can help in confidence.

**If the policy is not followed.** Repeated non-compliance is handled in steps: a verbal warning, then a written warning, and for continued breaches further action under the code of conduct. A single missed day is normally resolved with a conversation.

Managers can ask a team to come in on other days for planning or customer needs, with at least 2 working days' notice.

## Request to work from another city

```yaml

```

You may work from a city other than Bengaluru or Pune for up to **20 working days per leave year**, for example to be with family. The days do not need to be consecutive.

How to request:

1. At least **5 working days ahead**, open the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example and choose **Work location request**.
2. State the city, the dates and the reason, and confirm that you have a reliable internet connection and a private place to work.
3. Your manager approves it. The People Team is notified automatically and replies only if there is a problem.

Conditions:

- You must stay within India and keep to your normal working hours.
- You must be able to attend meetings by video and join in person if the team asks on core office days within 2 working days' notice (see HR-WFH-01, Hybrid schedule and core office days). Working from another city does not change your core-day obligations on days you are in the office.
- The company laptop and VPN are used as normal. Do not use a public network without the VPN.
- Travel and accommodation for the other city are your own cost and are not reimbursable.

More than 20 days in a year needs approval from your department head and the People Team. Extended periods may affect tax and payroll, so contact the People Team before asking for more than 20 days. Questions go to people@smartdesk.example.

## Working from abroad

```yaml

```

Working from another country is **not allowed without prior written approval** from both the People Team and Legal. This covers working remotely during a personal trip.

If approved, the limit is **15 working days per leave year** in a single country, and approval is given case by case.

Process:

1. Request at least **15 working days before** you plan to travel, through **Work location request** in the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example. Choose **Outside India**.
2. State the country, the dates and the reason.
3. The People Team checks tax, payroll and your right to work there; Legal checks data protection and customer contracts. IT Security checks that you will have a safe connection.
4. You receive the decision in writing. If it is a refusal, the reason is explained.

Interpretation points:

- Working a few hours of email while on holiday abroad still counts as working from abroad if you do it as part of your job.
- Some customer contracts and some countries prohibit access to certain data from outside India. In that case approval is declined however short the stay.
- Time spent on approved leave is not working from abroad. If you are on leave, do not work.
- Working abroad without approval is a breach of the code of conduct, and the cost of any tax or legal problems arising may be recovered.

Business expenses that have been approved are refunded through the expenses portal at https://expenses.smartdesk.example.

1. Sign in with your work account.
2. Choose **New claim** and add one line for each expense, with the date, amount, category and a short business reason.
3. Attach the receipt or invoice for each line. Receipts are mandatory for anything over **₹500**; for amounts at or below ₹500 a short note is enough.
4. Choose your approver (normally your manager) and submit.

Timing and payment:

- Submit within **30 days** of the spend. Claims older than 30 days need approval from your department head.
- Approved claims are paid with the next salary run if approved by the **20th** of the month. Later approvals are paid with the following month's salary.
- Reimbursements appear as a separate line on your payslip.

**Common reasons for rejection.** A missing or unreadable receipt, a category that does not match the receipt, or spend above the limits in HR-REIMB-02 (Travel and daily allowance limits). Claims in foreign currency need the card statement showing the rupee amount. Duplicate claims are rejected automatically.

If a claim is rejected, the approver adds a reason and you can correct and resubmit it. For payment problems, contact Finance at expenses@smartdesk.example.

## Travel and daily allowance limits

The following limits apply to approved business travel. Claims above them need approval from your department head before the trip.

- **Rail:** AC 2-tier or equivalent.
- **Local cabs and autos:** actual cost, with the app receipt. Use shared transport where it is reasonable to do so.
- **Hotels:** up to **₹7,500 per night** in Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad, and **₹5,000 per night** elsewhere, excluding taxes.

**Home internet: yes, but as a fixed monthly allowance.** Employees on the hybrid model receive **₹1,000 per month** towards a home internet connection. Claim it once a month in the expenses portal at https://expenses.smartdesk.example under the category **Home internet**, with a copy of that month's bill in your name or a family member's. **Gifts and entertainment.** You may accept occasional gifts or hospitality from customers or suppliers only if they are modest and do not influence your decisions. Anything worth more than **₹3,000** must be reported in the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example under **Compliance**, then **Gift register**, within 5 working days. Cash and cash equivalents may never be accepted. Never offer or accept anything in return for a favour: bribery is prohibited, whether in India or abroad.

**If you cannot decline.** If refusing a gift would cause offence because of local custom, you may accept it but must report it. Compliance decides whether you keep it, whether it is shared with your team, or whether it is returned or donated.

**Exceptions.** Any exception to this policy needs **prior written approval** from the Compliance Officer at compliance@smartdesk.example.

**Conflicts of interest.** A conflict exists when a personal interest could affect your judgement at work. Examples are owning part of a supplier or customer, employing a relative in your own team, or working for a competitor. You must disclose these in writing to your manager and the Compliance Officer as soon as you know. Outside work or side businesses need written approval first; you may not work for a competitor or use company time or equipment.

If unsure, ask Compliance. Failure to report is treated more seriously than the gift or interest itself. Concerns can also be raised through the process in HR-HARASS-02 (Report harassment and what happens next).

SmartDesk AI does not tolerate harassment of any kind. This includes sexual harassment, discrimination and bullying, whether it takes place in the office, at home, at a customer site, on a trip, by message or on a video call.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- unwelcome physical contact, comments, jokes or messages of a sexual nature;
- remarks or conduct based on gender, caste, religion, disability, age, marital status, sexual orientation or background;
- repeated shouting, humiliation, exclusion or spreading rumours;
- showing or sending offensive material.

What the company does:

- every employee completes **Anti-Harassment Awareness** within 14 days of joining and again each year;
- an **Internal Complaints Committee** receives and investigates complaints on sexual harassment, and the People Team deals with other forms;
- retaliation against anyone who complains in good faith, or who helps an investigation, is itself serious misconduct.

You do not need proof or a witness to raise a concern. Managers who learn of a possible incident must report it to the People Team within 24 hours, even if the employee asks them not to.

How to report a complaint is covered in HR-HARASS-02 (Report harassment and what happens next). Substantiated complaints lead to action from a formal warning up to dismissal, depending on severity.

The SmartDesk AI performance cycle follows the financial year, from **1 April to 31 March**.

Calendar:

- **By 30 April:** you and your manager agree your goals for the year in the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example under **Performance**.
- **October:** mid-year check-in. A conversation about progress, feedback and any changes to goals. There is no rating at this stage.
- **February:** self-assessment opens, where you write what you achieved against each goal. It closes on 28 February.
- **March:** your manager writes their assessment and discusses it with you. Ratings are calibrated across the department.
- **April:** you receive your final rating and outcome, together with salary and bonus decisions.

How it works: goals should be specific and measurable, with at most five. You can update goals during the year with your manager's agreement. Feedback from two or three colleagues is collected for the annual review, but your manager is responsible for the final assessment.

If you disagree with your rating, you can ask for a review by the next-level manager and the People Team within 10 working days of receiving it. The decision is final.

Questions go to the People Team at people@smartdesk.example or extension 4200.

## Rating scale and promotions

```yaml
id: HR-PERF-02
domain: HR
topic: performance
title: Rating scale and promotions
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-05-27
```

Performance is rated on a **5-point scale**:

1. **Needs improvement:** below the expected standard; a performance plan is agreed.
2. **Partially meets:** meets some of the goals, with clear gaps.
3. **Meets expectations:** reliable performance against the goals. Most employees are here.
4. **Exceeds expectations:** goes beyond the goals in scope or impact.
5. **Outstanding:** exceptional results that lift the whole team or business.

Ratings are calibrated across each department so that managers apply the same standard. As a guide, about 3% of employees get a 1, 7% a 2, 55% a 3, 28% a 4 and 7% a 5 (100% in total), but this is a guide, not a quota.

**Salary and bonus.** The rating feeds into the annual increase and the variable bonus. A rating of 1 or 2 usually means no increase.

**Promotions.** Promotions are considered twice a year, with effect from **1 April** and **1 October**. To be considered you need at least 12 months in your current role, a rating of 4 or above in the last cycle, and the nomination of your manager with supporting evidence. A nomination goes to a promotion panel for the department. Employees cannot nominate themselves, but can discuss their ambition with their manager at any time.

A promotion is confirmed with a letter in the people portal.

## Reviews if you joined or moved teams mid-cycle

```yaml

title: Reviews if you joined or moved teams mid-cycle
owner: People Team
```

**If you joined during the cycle.**

- Joined on or before **30 September**: you are rated for the cycle, but your goals and salary decision are pro-rated for the months you worked.
- Joined on or after **1 October**: you are not rated for that cycle. You still have a check-in conversation with your manager and goals for the rest of the year, and your first rating comes in the next cycle.
- If you are on probation when the cycle closes, your probation confirmation counts as your review.

**If you changed manager or team during the cycle.**

- Your final rating is written by your **current** manager, but they must collect written input from your previous manager on the period you worked together.
- If you spent more than 6 months in the previous team, the previous manager's input has equal weight on the part of the year that is theirs.
- Goals from the old team that you did not complete are agreed with both managers.

**If you were away for a long period.** Extended leave, such as maternity leave, does not lower your rating. Your goals are adjusted for the months you worked, and the rating is based on those months.

For borderline dates, such as joining on 1 October, the People Team makes the decision. Contact them at people@smartdesk.example if your case is unclear.
