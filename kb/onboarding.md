Your first day starts at 09:30 at reception in the Bengaluru or Pune office. Remote joiners join a video orientation at 10:00 IST; the link is in the welcome email sent 3 working days before your start date.

On day one, work through this list:

1. Meet the People Team (extension 4200) to sign your contract documents, the confidentiality agreement and the code of conduct acknowledgement.
2. Hand in your bank details and tax details in the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example, so that your first salary is not delayed.
3. Collect your laptop and kit from the IT counter, or unpack the courier delivery if you are remote. See IT-EQUIP-01 (What equipment a new joiner receives).
4. Sign in to your laptop, then set up multi-factor authentication at https://id.smartdesk.example/mfa.
5. Install Outlook on your phone if you want work email there.
6. Meet your manager for the first-week plan, and meet your buddy.
7. Start the two mandatory courses in the people portal: Security Awareness (about 45 minutes) and Anti-Harassment Awareness (about 30 minutes). Both must be finished within 14 days.

**If something is missing.** If your laptop is not ready, call IT on extension 4100; you can start with orientation and collect it later in the day. If you cannot sign in to the people portal, ask the People Team to resend your temporary password.

Your benefits enrolment window opens on day one and closes after 30 days. Do not leave it to the last week.

## Enrol in benefits

```yaml

```

All permanent employees are covered by a benefits package. You must enrol in the people portal within **30 days of joining**.

What is included:

- **Health insurance:** a family floater cover of ₹5,00,000, covering you, your spouse and up to two children. Parents can be added at your own cost.
- **Group life insurance:** 3 times your annual fixed pay.
- **Personal accident cover:** ₹10,00,000, covering you only.
- **NPS contribution (optional):** an employer-matched contribution up to 5% of basic pay, if you choose to take part.

How to enrol:

1. Sign in at https://people.smartdesk.example and open **Benefits**, then **Enrol now**.
2. Add each family member you want covered, with name, date of birth and relationship.
3. Review the cost summary. Any additional-parent premium is deducted from salary.
4. Submit. A confirmation email is sent, and insurance cards are available in the portal within 5 working days.

**If you miss the 30-day window.** You can still enrol, but only during the annual open window each January, unless your family situation changes through marriage, birth or adoption of a child. You then have 30 days from that date to add the person. Questions go to benefits@smartdesk.example.

## The buddy programme

```yaml

domain: HR
topic: buddy
title: The buddy programme
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-05-04
```

Every new joiner is paired with a **buddy** for their first 90 days. The buddy is an experienced colleague from a different team, so you have someone to ask the questions that feel too small for your manager. Your buddy's name is in your welcome email.

What a buddy does:

- holds a 30-minute check-in every week for the first six weeks, then every two weeks until day 90;
- explains how things really work, such as which channel to ask in and who to contact for what;
- introduces you to people outside your own team;
- listens, and points you to the right person if the question is about pay, leave or a problem with your manager.

What a buddy does not do: assess your performance, approve anything, or report on you to your manager. The conversations are informal and are not recorded in any file.

**If it is not working.** If your buddy leaves, changes roles or you simply do not get on, tell the People Team on extension 4200 or at people@smartdesk.example and a new buddy is assigned within 3 working days. No reason needs to be given.

After 90 days, many pairs stay in touch, but the formal programme ends. Buddies are volunteers; if you would like to be one, tell the People Team.

## Probation: length, reviews and extension

```yaml
id: HR-PROB-01
domain: HR
topic: probation
title: "Probation: length, reviews and extension"
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-06-15
```

New permanent employees serve a **probation of 6 months** from the date of joining. During probation, your manager reviews your work at three points: at 30 days, at 90 days and a confirmation review in month 5.

How confirmation works:

1. The People Team sends your manager a confirmation form 30 days before your probation ends.
2. Your manager rates your performance and recommends one of three outcomes: confirm, extend or end employment.
3. You receive the outcome by the last day of probation. If confirmed, a confirmation letter is posted in the people portal.

Probation can be **extended once, by up to 3 months**, if there are clear gaps against agreed goals. The extension is explained to you in writing, with the specific goals to meet.

Notice period: **15 days** during probation, either way. After confirmation it becomes **60 days**.

If you hear nothing by the last day of probation, do not assume you are confirmed. Contact the People Team on extension 4200. Your probation is not complete until the confirmation letter appears.

Contract employees follow the terms in their own contract instead.

## Taking leave during probation

```yaml

domain: HR
topic: probation
title: Taking leave during probation
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-08-03
```

Probation does not stop you taking leave, but some leave types work differently while it lasts.

- **Casual leave and sick leave:** available from day one. Casual leave is pro-rated for the part of the year you are employed.
- **Earned leave:** it accrues from your first month at 1.5 days per month, but you cannot use it until you are confirmed. In a genuine emergency, your manager and the People Team can approve earned leave in advance, using what you have accrued so far.
- **Parental leave:** subject to its own eligibility rules, not to confirmation. See HR-LEAVE-06 (Maternity leave) and HR-LEAVE-07 (Paternity and adoption leave).

**How absence affects probation.** If your total unplanned absence during probation exceeds 15 working days, your probation is normally extended by the number of days over 15, so the reviewers have enough time to assess you. Approved sick leave with a medical certificate for a serious illness can be treated as an exception; the People Team decides.

**Interpretation example.** If you join on 1 June and take 3 casual days in July, those days do not change your probation end date. If you are away for 20 working days in total, your probation could be extended by 5 days.

Applying for leave works the same way as for everyone else; see HR-LEAVE-04 (Apply for leave in the people portal).

## What equipment a new joiner receives

Every new employee receives the following on or before day one:

- a company laptop (a 14-inch standard model, or a 16-inch high-performance model for engineering and data roles);
- a power adapter and carry sleeve;
- a USB-C docking station;
- a wireless keyboard and mouse;
- a managed user account, with access to SmartDesk Authenticator for multi-factor authentication.

The laptop arrives with disk encryption, the VPN client and the standard software already installed, so you can sign in and start working.

**How it is arranged.** The People Team confirms your start date to IT at least 5 working days ahead, and IT prepares the kit. On day one you collect it from the IT counter on the ground floor (Bengaluru) or the IT store (Pune). Remote employees outside these cities receive the same kit by courier; the tracking details are sent to the personal email address you gave when accepting the offer.

If your role needs a different laptop model, for example for specialised data work, your manager requests it before your start date at https://helpdesk.smartdesk.example.

Contractors receive a laptop only when their contract says so. Equipment stays the property of SmartDesk AI and must be returned when you leave.

## Request extra peripherals

```yaml

owner: IT Service Desk
last_reviewed: 2026-04-06
```

Beyond the standard kit, you can request a keyboard, mouse, headset or a spare USB-C dock.

1. Open https://helpdesk.smartdesk.example and choose **New request**, then **Equipment**.
2. Select the item from the catalogue and give a short business reason.
3. Your manager approves it by email.
4. IT prepares the item and notifies you. Delivery takes up to 5 working days. In Pune you collect it from the IT store; in Bengaluru, from the IT counter on the ground floor. Remote employees receive it by courier.

**Limits.** Headsets are limited to one per employee every 3 years, but a replacement for a worn-out one can be requested sooner if you return the old headset. Items that are not in the catalogue need approval from your cost-centre owner and a quote from Procurement.

**Ergonomic needs.** If you need equipment for health or ergonomic reasons, tell the People Team (people@smartdesk.example) first. HR handles these requests separately and in confidence, and does not need you to give your manager medical details. IT then provides the approved item.

Do not buy peripherals yourself expecting a refund; purchases made without approval are not reimbursed.

## Lost or stolen laptop

```yaml
id: IT-EQUIP-03
domain: IT
topic: equipment
title: Lost or stolen laptop
owner: IT Security
last_reviewed: 2026-09-07
```

If your laptop is lost or stolen, act in this order:

1. **Call Security on extension 4999 straight away**, at any hour. IT locks the device remotely and signs you out everywhere. Disk encryption protects the data, but your account still has to be secured.
2. **Report the theft to the police** if it was stolen, and keep the report number.
3. Raise a request at https://helpdesk.smartdesk.example within 24 hours with the date, place and what happened.
4. IT issues a replacement, usually within 2 working days.
5. Change your password at https://id.smartdesk.example/reset once you have the replacement.

**Interpretation points.**

- Prompt reporting matters most. Employees who report within 24 hours are not charged where the loss was not due to carelessness.
- If a laptop is lost through negligence, for example left unattended in a vehicle, the People Team and your manager review the case. The employee may be asked to cover part of the replacement cost, up to 50% of the depreciated value (company policy).
- Never wait to see whether the laptop turns up before reporting it. If it is found after you reported it, return it to IT; do not use it.
- If the laptop was lost while travelling on business, say so in the request. This is considered when deciding on any charge.

## Returning equipment when you leave

```yaml
id: IT-EQUIP-04
domain: IT
topic: equipment
title: Returning equipment when you leave
owner: IT Service Desk
last_reviewed: 2026-10-12
```

On your last working day, return all company equipment: laptop, charger, dock, keyboard, mouse, headset and any hardware security key.

1. Once your resignation is accepted, raise an **Offboarding: equipment return** request at https://helpdesk.smartdesk.example.
2. Back up any personal files you are allowed to keep. IT does not recover or return personal files after the laptop is collected, and company data must stay on company systems.
3. Return the items to the IT counter (Bengaluru, ground floor) or the IT store (Pune) by 17:00 on your last day. Remote employees receive a prepaid courier label by email and must send the package so it arrives within 3 working days.
4. IT checks the items and signs off the return in the people portal.

Your account is disabled at 18:00 on your last working day, and email forwarding is not provided.

**Why it matters.** Your final settlement (see HR-PAY-01, Pay-day schedule and holiday exceptions) is released only after IT confirms the return, so return everything on time. Missing or unreturned items are valued at their depreciated cost and deducted from the settlement.

If you cannot return in person on your last day, for instance because you are on leave, agree a different date with your manager and IT beforehand. The date is recorded in the request.
