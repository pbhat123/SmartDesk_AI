## Pay-day schedule and holiday exceptions


Salaries are paid monthly, on the **last working day of the month**, directly to the bank account registered in the people portal. The payslip is published in the portal by the **2nd** of the following month.

Exceptions and details:

- If the last day of the month falls on a weekend or a public holiday, salary is paid on the **previous working day**.
- Bank transfers usually arrive on the same day, but can take up to one more working day, depending on your bank.
- The payroll cut-off for changes such as new bank details or tax declaration changes is the **20th** of each month. Changes received after that apply from the following month.
- **New joiners:** if you join on or before the 20th, you are paid pro-rata for the days worked in your first month with that month's salary. If you join after the 20th, those days are paid with the next month's salary.
- **Leavers:** the final settlement, including unused earned leave and any deductions, is paid within 45 days of your last working day, after the equipment return in IT-EQUIP-04 (Returning equipment when you leave) is confirmed.

If your salary has not arrived by the second working day of the month, check your payslip first and then contact Payroll: extension 4250, direct line +91-80-5550-0250, or payroll@smartdesk.example. Please include your employee ID and the amount you expected.

Payslips are available in the people portal for the current and all earlier months.

1. Sign in at https://people.smartdesk.example.
2. Open **Pay**, then **Payslips**.
3. Select the month and choose **Download PDF**.
4. Open the PDF with your **employee ID in capital letters** as the password.

Your payslip shows earnings (basic, HRA, special allowance and any bonus or reimbursement), deductions (provident fund, income tax deducted at source, and any recovery) and the net amount paid. Your year-to-date totals appear at the bottom.

**Common problems.**

- If the month you want is missing after the 2nd, Payroll has not yet released it; contact payroll@smartdesk.example.
- If the PDF will not open, check that you are using capital letters for the employee ID and that your PDF reader is up to date.
- If a figure looks wrong, for example a missing reimbursement or unexpected tax, raise it at https://helpdesk.smartdesk.example with the category **Payroll**, or call Payroll on extension 4250.

You can also download your annual tax summary from the same **Pay** section under **Tax documents** once the financial year closes. Save copies of your payslips if you leave, because portal access ends with your employment.


Payroll deducts income tax at source each month, based on your declared choices for the year. The following is how to submit your tax declaration; it is not tax advice.

1. **Choose your regime by 30 April.** In the people portal, open **Pay**, then **Tax declaration**, and choose the old or new tax regime. If you do not choose, the new regime is applied.
2. **Declare your investments and claims** if you chose the old regime, such as insurance premiums and home loan interest. You can edit the declaration until **31 January**.
3. **House rent:** if you claim rent allowance, enter your landlord's details, and the PAN of the landlord if the annual rent is above ₹1,00,000.
4. **Submit proof between 1 February and 15 March.** Upload receipts and certificates in the same screen. Declarations without proof are not accepted.
5. Payroll reviews the proof and updates the tax for the final months of the year.

If you miss the proof deadline, tax is recalculated and deducted at the higher rate from the March salary. You can still claim any missing deduction yourself when you file your own return.

New joiners declare within 30 days of joining, and tell Payroll about tax already deducted by a previous employer using the previous employer's tax certificate. For questions, contact payroll@smartdesk.example or extension 4250.

## Salary structure explained

```yaml
id: HR-SAL-01
domain: HR
topic: salary
title: Salary structure explained
owner: Payroll
last_reviewed: 2026-05-18
```

Your offer letter states your **annual CTC** (cost to company). It is split into these components:

- **Basic pay:** **40% of CTC**. Many other items are calculated from it.
- **House rent allowance (HRA):** **20% of CTC**, paid monthly. It can reduce tax if you pay rent and chose the old regime.
- **Special allowance:** the balance of the fixed pay after basic, HRA and the other items below.
- **Employer provident fund:** the company pays **12% of basic** into your provident fund. You contribute a matching 12%, which is deducted from your pay.
- **Group insurance:** the cost of the health, life and accident cover the company provides to you.
- **Annual performance bonus:** a variable amount, paid with the June salary, based on the rating in the performance review and the company's results. The target is shown in your offer letter as a percentage of fixed pay.

**Monthly take-home** is the fixed pay after deductions: your provident fund contribution, income tax at source and, if applicable, extra benefit premiums. This is why your take-home is lower than CTC divided by 12.

To see your own breakdown, open **Pay**, then **Compensation** in the people portal at https://people.smartdesk.example. Questions about how a figure is calculated go to Payroll at payroll@smartdesk.example.

## Stock-option vesting schedule

```yaml
last_reviewed: 2026-07-06
```

Eligible employees receive stock options as part of their offer or after a promotion or strong review. The grant letter states the number of options and the exercise price.

**Vesting schedule: 4 years with a 1-year cliff.**

- Nothing vests during the first 12 months.
- At the **1-year anniversary** (the cliff), **25%** of the grant vests at once.
- The remaining **75%** vests in **36 equal monthly instalments**, on the same date each month, for the following three years. That is 1/48 of the grant per month after the cliff.
- The grant is fully vested after 4 years.

Vesting starts from your **grant date**, which is normally your joining date for joiner grants. For grants after a promotion, it is the date in the grant letter.

**Checking your options.** Sign in at https://people.smartdesk.example and open **Equity**, where you can see the number granted, vested and unvested, and the exercise price. Vested options can be exercised only during an approved exercise window, announced by email.

Vesting continues during approved paid leave, including parental leave. Unpaid leave of more than 30 days pauses vesting for that period.

Options are not salary, and their value depends on the share price. Questions go to the People Team on extension 4200 or at equity@smartdesk.example.

## Stock options when you resign or are laid off

```yaml
id: HR-STOCK-02
domain: HR
topic: stock options
title: Stock options when you resign or are laid off
owner: People Team
last_reviewed: 2026-09-30
```

What happens to your options depends on how much has vested when your employment ends.

- **Vested options** can be exercised within **90 days** of your last working day. You pay the exercise price and any tax due. After 90 days they lapse.
- **Unvested options** lapse on your last working day. Vesting does not continue after you leave.
- **Notice period counts.** Vesting continues during your notice period, so options that vest during the 60 days are yours to exercise.

Interpretation points:

- **Resigning before the 1-year cliff:** you have no vested options, so nothing can be exercised. Resigning one day before the cliff gives you nothing; one day after gives you 25%.
- **Layoff:** the treatment is the same as resignation, except that if the notice is paid in lieu of working, vesting counts up to the end of the notice period.
- **Dismissal for serious misconduct:** all options, including vested ones, can be forfeited by decision of the board.
- **Moving to a role with no options:** vested options stay yours. Unvested ones continue to vest while you are employed.

To exercise, use **Equity**, then **Exercise** in the people portal before the 90 days end. Payment of the exercise price and tax is made by bank transfer. Leavers lose portal access after their last day, so exercise requests are made by email to equity@smartdesk.example. Contact the People Team on extension 4200 with questions.
